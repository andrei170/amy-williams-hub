The Accelerator is mentorship, not regulated business or financial advice. The build stays carefully on the educator + mentor side, the same way your existing courses do. Guardrails we apply:

  • No income guarantees and no "six-figure studio" promises in copy
  • "Tuition for serious working artists" hint copy only on the landing, figure confirmed on the discovery call
